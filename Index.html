Bas Intezaar Hai
Tumhara

♥

— ek dil ki awaaz —

Shaam ki halki si roshni mein,

jab din dheere-dheere dhalta hai,

mere dil ke kisi kone mein

bas tumhara khayal jalta hai…

Khidki par thehri hawa bhi,

jaise tumhari khushboo poochti hai,

aur chaand ki narm si roshni

tumhari kami mehsoos karti hai…

Tum door ho, apni duty mein masroof,

aur main yahan tumhari raah dekhti hoon,

har aahat ko tum samajh kar,

chupke se darwaaze ki taraf dekhti hoon…

❤   ❤   ❤

Bas intezaar hai tumhara…

Kab aaoge mere paas,

kab meri ungliyon ko thaam kar

mujhe apne kareeb bulaoge…

Kab mere sar ko

apne seene par rakh kar,

mere bikhre baalon mein

pyaar se ungliyan chalaoge…

Kuch kehna nahi hai tumse,

bas tumhara paas hona hai…

Tumhari baahon ki narmi mein

thodi der yunhi kho jaana hai…

❤   ❤   ❤

✦ Mere Patidev ✦

ye dil koi shikayat nahi karta,

bas tumhari ek jhalak ke liye

har pal dheere se dhadakta hai…

Jab tum aaoge na,

shayad main kuch keh na paaun,

bas aankhon mein halki si nami,

aur honton par ek muskaan hogi…

Phir tum mujhe baahon mein bhar lena,

aur main aankhein band karke

sirf itna mehsoos karungi —

"

Haan… ab intezaar khatam hua,
mera sukoon mere paas laut aaya. ❤️🫂
